# Local Almaty recommendation photographs

Five landscape photographs are bundled offline. Source pages, authors, licenses,
actual depicted locations, dimensions and checksums are in `photo_sources.json`.
In-app credits are available from the HOME sheet. CC BY-SA photographs and their
resized WebP derivatives retain CC BY-SA 4.0; the lake photograph is CC BY 3.0.

Assets: `big_almaty_lake.webp`, `butakovsky_waterfall.webp`, `kok_zhailau.webp`,
`furmanov_peak.webp`, `medeu_shymbulak.webp`. Maximum size 800 × 600 px,
WebP quality 72. No hotlinks or image API is used by Android.

`furmanov_peak.webp` shows the approach to the peak, not a confirmed view of its summit;
this is marked as `approach` in metadata and explained on the card in both locales.
`butakovsky_waterfall.webp` shows the lower waterfall and its rocky surroundings.

No verified photographs were installed for Three Brothers or Tourist Peak.
Those cards remain text-only. Do not copy another location's photo to
`tri_bratya.webp` or `tourist_peak.webp`.

To replace/reinstall a photograph, download it from the corresponding source page,
convert to actual WebP (renaming JPEG is insufficient), keep the filename and
update metadata and PHOTO_CREDITS.txt. Rebuild the APK after modifying assets.
